Undoubtedly the mall with the best location in Dasmarinas, Cavite, Robinsons Dasmarinas takes pride in providing a value shopping experience within reach to over half a million residents of Dasmarinas and over a million commuters who traverse this intersection daily. This intersection in Sitio Palapala is very accessible to key towns and cities such as GMA, Carmona, and Tagaytay City.

Robinsons Place Dasmarinas is a 3-level shopping haven featuring over 150 retail shops, dining outlets, entertainment facilities and service centers. Offering a variety of choices from shopping to dining to entertainment and recreation, Robinsons Place Dasmarinas perfectly captures the upscale lifestyle of this highly urbanized city. As an added advantage, the mall utilizes up-to-date building facilities to provide easy access, comfort and convenience to all its shoppers and mall goers.

All ages are allowed inside the mall.

Shop for your favorites and stock up on your essentials daily at Robinsons Dasmariñas.
