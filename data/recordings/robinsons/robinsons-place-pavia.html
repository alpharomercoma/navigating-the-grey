Shining against the backdrop of a progressive municipality, Robinsons Pavia was built as a new shopping destination situated at the center of Pavia Iloilo. Its design reflects the festive spirits of Pavianhons. The glass façade tells of their personality, while its alternating earth-toned palette gives a warm and welcoming impression. The harmonious weaving of steel, aluminum, glass, stone, and wood embodies the fiesta culture. Everyone will feel welcome here, whatever walk of life one may come from. On top of this is a wonderful aquatic park that kids and kids at heart must visit. With 12 water attractions including pool playground, octopus, sea horse, and jelly fish sprinklers, water pillars, giant water buckets and slides. It is located at the right side of the mall's entrance that is surrounded by cabanas where guests can enjoy and relax.

All ages are allowed inside the mall.

Shop for your favorites and stock up on your essentials daily at Robinsons Pavia.
