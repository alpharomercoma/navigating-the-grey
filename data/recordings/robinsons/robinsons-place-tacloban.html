Recently awarded as the 2010 Shopping Center of the Year (Small Category) by the Philippine Retailer's Association, Robinsons Tacloban continues to serve over 2 million Filipinos in Eastern Visayas. Conveniently located along Tabuan National Highway in Marasbaras, Tacloban City, it showcases a unique and exciting tenant mix which offers an exceptional line of delectable cuisines, trendy apparel, comfy footwear, modern gadgets and electronics, as well as service outlets. As a special treat for its valued patrons and visitors, it has also upgraded movie-watching experience in the region through its five (5) state of the art cinemas that include a 3D cinema - the first in Region 8. Since it started its operations in June 2009, Robinsons Tacloban takes pride in bringing in a steady flow of customer traffic, which makes it an ideal venue in launching new products, new outlets and in staging groundbreaking marketing events and promotions in the region. Indeed, it has changed the lifestyle of the Filipinos in this province by providing world-class malling experience within their reach.

All ages are welcome inside the mall.

Shop for your favorites and stock up on your essentials daily at Robinsons Tacloban.
